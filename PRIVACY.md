# Privacy Policy

**PromptPen** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. We operate entirely within your browser and only communicate with the AI provider you explicitly configure.

## Data Processing

### Text you select
When you select text on a webpage and click an action button, the selected text is sent **directly from your browser** to the AI provider you configured (e.g., OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google). This is required to perform the requested action (rewrite, summarize, translate, etc.).

- The text is never sent to PromptPen's servers — we have none.
- The text is processed solely by your chosen AI provider under their terms and privacy policy.
- You control what text is sent and when.

### API keys
Your API keys are stored locally in your browser using the `chrome.storage.local` API. They are only transmitted to the AI provider you configured when making a request. PromptPen never accesses or stores your API keys externally.

### Settings and preferences
All extension settings (theme preference, custom prompts, website access rules, tour state) are stored locally in your browser. They never leave your device.

## Data Collection

PromptPen does **not** collect:

- Personal information (name, email, address)
- Browsing history or web activity
- Login credentials or authentication data
- Keystrokes, clicks, mouse position, or scroll events
- Page content beyond what you actively select and submit
- Diagnostic or usage analytics

## Third-Party Data Sharing

The only third party that receives data is the **AI provider you choose and configure** (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.). We do not control their data handling practices — refer to their respective privacy policies for details.

PromptPen does not share data with any advertising networks, analytics services, or other third parties.

## Data Retention

Since no data is collected by PromptPen, nothing is retained. API keys and settings remain in your browser's local storage until you uninstall the extension or clear browser data.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this document and bump the extension version. Material changes will be noted in the release notes.
